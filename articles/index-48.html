Today, I’m excited to share my journey of earning a Hall of Fame recognition from McDonald’s through Bugcrowd 🍟🍔

🔍 Getting Started

McDonald’s runs a Vulnerability Disclosure Program (VDP) on Bugcrowd, so I started with reconnaissance by gathering subdomains of their domain.

I used GoWitness 📸 to capture screenshots of all discovered subdomains and began reviewing them one by one.

🧪 The Discovery

During my exploration, I found a login page 🔐. Initially, testing didn’t reveal anything critical.

However, while analyzing the forgot password functionality, I discovered a username enumeration vulnerability.

The issue was:

  • ⚠️ The application revealed whether a user account exists
  • ❌ It showed different responses for valid and invalid users
  • ✅ Instead of using a generic response like “If the account exists, you will receive an OTP”

⚠️ Impact

Although classified as a low-severity vulnerability, this issue can help attackers identify valid user accounts.

Such information can be leveraged in further attacks like brute force or phishing campaigns.

As a security researcher 🛡️, I strongly believe:

Every vulnerability matters — from informational (P5) to critical (P1)

Even small issues can become dangerous when chained together 🔗💥

📩 Reporting

I responsibly reported the vulnerability through Bugcrowd without exploiting it.

  • ✅ The report was accepted as Low Severity
  • 🏅 I received a Hall of Fame recognition
  • Hall Of Fame: https://bugcrowd.com/engagements/mcdonalds/hall_of_fames
