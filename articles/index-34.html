I hope you all are doing well! 😄 Today, I want to share my personal journey—how a small village boy became recognized by well-reputed companies and government organizations, and even featured in the newspaper Voice of Day, Gujarat. 📰✨

My Journey 🚀

At the age of 17, I got my first smartphone after finishing 12th grade. Before that, my knowledge about technology was minimal—I only used old Nokia and Intex phones to browse Google or download songs. Internet charges were ₹20 for just 30 MB per month! 💸

One day, I watched a YouTube video about Ethical Hacking. I had always thought hacking was illegal, but learning that ethical hackers help companies and governments protect themselves fascinated me. 🛡️💻

Though eager, I lacked foundational knowledge. I had completed one year of a Bachelor of Commerce degree when COVID-19 hit in 2020. 😷🏠

The Golden Opportunity 🌟

I didn’t have a computer, but one day, my childhood school teacher asked me to fix a computer issue. After solving it, I was granted access to the school’s Wi-Fi and computers—a golden chance! 🙏

I dedicated myself to learning computer fundamentals, Linux, networking, and programming. I started with Python to create my first tools and programs. A full year of focused learning transformed my skills.

By the time I finished my degree, I was ready to dive into ethical hacking, cybersecurity, web security, and application security. Later, I pursued an MCA degree while earning income through bug bounties. 💰

Achievements & Recognition 🏆

At 20, I earned my first ₹5000 from Upstox—an unforgettable moment! 🌕 Since then, I have worked on numerous bug bounties, collaborating with NASA, Apple, Mercedes, the Dutch government, Government of India, HostGator, Upstox, and more.

By 22, I was featured in Voice of Day, Gujarat. My village, Karmal Pipaliya, and my family felt immense pride. I also began delivering lectures on ethical hacking and cybersecurity at my MCA college, inspiring the next generation. 🎓💡
