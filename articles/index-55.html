Today, I want to share how I discovered a security issue on HP's website and received an acknowledgment from their security team 🏆💙

As an HP Victus gaming laptop user, receiving recognition from a company whose products I use personally was a very special moment for me 😊

🚀 The Discovery

During my bug-hunting journey, I was performing reconnaissance and content discovery on one of HP's web applications 🔍⚡

While testing the target, I used a custom wordlist that I created through my own research and reconnaissance efforts 🛠️

After performing enumeration, I discovered a publicly accessible .php file containing sensitive installation-related information 🚨

⚠️ Security Impact

The information exposed within the file should not have been publicly available and could potentially assist attackers in understanding internal application configurations 🔓

Exposed installation files and configuration-related resources often reveal sensitive details that can increase an application's attack surface ⚠️

📩 Responsible Disclosure

After validating the finding, I responsibly reported the issue to HP through their official security disclosure email 📧🛡️ found at here: https://enable.hp.com/potentialsecurityvulnerability-report

The HP security team reviewed the report and worked to remediate the issue ✅

After the fix was implemented, they confirmed the resolution and acknowledged my contribution toward improving the security of their platform 🎖️🎉

🏅 Key Takeaways

  • 🔍 Reconnaissance is one of the most important phases of bug hunting
  • 🛠️ Custom wordlists can help uncover hidden assets and endpoints
  • 📂 Misconfigured installation files can expose sensitive information
  • 📩 Responsible disclosure helps organizations improve security
  • 🚀 Consistent learning and testing can lead to valuable findings

💡 Advice for Hunters

Never underestimate simple misconfigurations during reconnaissance 👀

Sometimes a seemingly small exposed file can reveal valuable information and lead to impactful security findings 🔥
