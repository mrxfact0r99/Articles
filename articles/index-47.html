Today, I want to share how I discovered a security vulnerability in Rapido and responsibly disclosed it to their team 🚕⚡

🔍 The Discovery

While casually exploring Rapido’s website, my curiosity as a security researcher and ethical hacker kicked in 🤔. During my testing, I came across a subdomain and decided to perform directory brute-forcing.

During this process, I discovered a configuration file that exposed sensitive information such as:

  • 🚨 API Keys
  • 🔑 Tokens
  • 🛠️ Credentials

This was a serious case of:

  • ⚠️ Sensitive Information Disclosure
  • ⚙️ Security Misconfiguration

Since the issue is currently being fixed, I won’t disclose specific technical details or file names.

🏢 Responsible Disclosure

As an ethical hacker and bug bounty hunter, I immediately reported the vulnerability responsibly to the Rapido team without exploiting the issue.

Responsible disclosure is a crucial part of ethical hacking our goal is always to protect users and strengthen systems, not misuse vulnerabilities 🔒

✅ Response & Acknowledgment

After submitting the report, I was genuinely happy to receive acknowledgment from the Rapido team 🙌

In many cases especially in India 🇮🇳 companies silently fix vulnerabilities, mark reports as duplicates, or sometimes don’t even respond.

However, Rapido stood out. Despite not having a public bug bounty program, their team appreciated the report and is actively working on fixing the issue 💯

This reflects their professionalism and shows how seriously they take user data protection and digital security 🔐

🛡️ Final Thoughts

Always follow ethical hacking principles. If you discover a vulnerability, report it responsibly and give the organization time to fix it.

Together, we can make the internet a safer place 🌍🔐
