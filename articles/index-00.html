Why I Had to Leave Medium & The Story Behind Creating My Own Website ✨🚀📖

Apr 1, 2025 3 min read Utsavpari Gosai

Hello, fellow hackers! 🐱‍💻

I hope you're all doing well. First off, I want to apologize for my long absence from Medium. There’s a solid reason behind it, and today, I’m going to share the full story of why I had to move on and create my own personal website.

🚨 The Issue with My Old Account

For those who’ve been following me for a while, you probably remember my original Medium account: gosaiutsav5. I used it to share bug hunting stories, cybersecurity tips, and personal thoughts. Everything was going great until January, when things suddenly took a turn.

I had joined the Medium Partner Program just out of curiosity. One of my bug hunting stories gained a lot of attention — tons of views and some amazing, supportive comments. It even earned $1 in a single day! 💰 That was exciting. But then, out of nowhere, Medium suspended my account, saying I had violated their rules.

📢 My Attempts to Fix It

I was shocked. I knew I hadn't broken any rules, so I contacted Medium multiple times. I sent emails, submitted appeals, and clearly explained that I wasn’t even concerned about monetization — I just wanted to share knowledge with the hacking and cybersecurity community.

Sadly, I never got a clear explanation or a positive response. After waiting three months with no resolution, I felt incredibly disappointed with Medium for what felt like unfair treatment.

🚨 The New Account... and Another Roadblock

So, I created a new account: mrxfact0r99. I started re-uploading my old stories. But here's the problem — Medium limits uploads to just 3 stories per day. I managed to upload 10 stories over a few days... and then boom — they suspended this new account too.

That was it for me. I felt frustrated and heartbroken. I didn’t want to go through the same situation again and again.

🔥 A Fresh Start — My Own Website

That’s when I had the idea — why not build my own space, with no restrictions or gatekeepers? A place where I could freely share bug bounty stories, cybersecurity content, and personal experiences with the community.

So that’s what I did. 💪 This new website is now my home base for everything I create — and I’m excited to make it better than ever before. Whether you’re a beginner, intermediate, or advanced security enthusiast, this platform will have something valuable for you.

Thank you all for your continued support throughout this journey. This is just the beginning of a new chapter — and it’s going to be awesome! 🔥✨

👉 Make sure to follow this website to stay up to date with my latest posts and projects.

💬 If you ever have questions, need help, or just want to chat, feel free to reach out!
