How I Got Acknowledged in Apple’s Hall of Fame (2nd Time) 🏅

Oct 19, 2025 3 min read Utsavpari Gosai

Hello Hackers 👋

I hope everyone’s doing great! 🙏 It’s been an incredible journey lately, and I’m super excited to share that I’ve been acknowledged by Apple’s Security Hall of Fame — for the second time! 🎉

For those who don’t know, I was previously listed in Apple’s Hall of Fame earlier this year. You can check out my first acknowledgment story Here.

Recently, while going through a newspaper interview Here , I decided to revisit Apple’s official Hall of Fame page at support.apple.com/en-us/102774. Out of curiosity, I searched for my name — and to my surprise, I found it not once, but twice! Once in March 2025 (which I already knew about), and again in April 2025 😱

After checking my submission reports, I realized that I had actually reported two different vulnerabilities to Apple. Both were valid — one accepted earlier and another one accepted later — which led to this double acknowledgment.

Honestly, I didn’t even notice when the second one got listed, so it was an amazing surprise to find it while browsing!

I’m deeply grateful to the Apple Security Team for recognizing my efforts once again. Getting acknowledged by one of the world’s most secure and respected tech companies is a true honor.

Here’s the official Apple acknowledgment page where you can find my name listed twice: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102774

A huge thank you to my family, my close friends, and my inspiration Hacker Shiva — your encouragement keeps me going every single day 🙌
