Hello hackers,

I hope you’re all doing well 🙏 First of all, I apologize for not uploading articles on Medium — all my accounts were suspended. That’s why I created my own website to continue sharing knowledge freely. You can read the full story about that here.

Now let’s talk about one of the proudest moments in my hacking journey: getting acknowledged by Apple in their official Hall of Fame! 🏆

This is not the first time I was acknowledged — I was also honored by NASA (Government Of USA) And NPIIC (Government Of India). (You can read that story here and here too!) But this time, it was by Apple — one of the most secure and respected tech companies in the world, which makes it a huge achievement for me.

While I can't disclose exact details due to Apple’s security policy, I can say it was an injection vulnerability found on one of their subdomains. I reported it back in January. In March, they responded saying the issue was fixed and asked me to verify. I confirmed the fix, and they told me my name would appear in the next update of their Hall of Fame.

And yes — in April 2025, I found my name on their official page. A lifetime achievement unlocked! 🔐
