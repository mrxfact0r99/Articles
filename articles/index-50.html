But yes I was able to discover something important 🔥

As usual, I started with subdomain enumeration 🔍 using prebuilt wordlists along with my personal custom wordlists.

I fingerprinted technologies, checked inputs and application flows through Burp Suite, and during testing, I came across a WordPress endpoint.

🧪 The Discovery

I found the following endpoint on one subdomain:

/wp-json/wp/v2/users/

This endpoint was leaking user information, including superuser details, admin names, metadata, and some PII-related information ⚠️

Many companies do not accept this type of issue, but in this case, I could clearly see sensitive administrative information being exposed.

I strongly believed it was worth reporting responsibly 📩

📩 Reporting & Recognition

And yes NVIDIA accepted the report ✅

In my case, it was marked as Medium Severity, but what impressed me the most was that NVIDIA also acknowledges even Low severity bugs through their Hall of Fame and values researchers who help improve security 🙌
