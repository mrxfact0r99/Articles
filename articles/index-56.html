🇷🇺 An Unexpected Discovery

As many of you may know, the Russian Government does not publicly operate a bug bounty program. Based on my research, only security researchers and bug hunters from Russia are generally permitted to test and report vulnerabilities on their systems.

However, while conducting security research, I discovered an information disclosure issue on a Russian Government website 🌐⚠️

The issue exposed information that should not have been publicly accessible and could potentially provide useful intelligence to malicious actors.

Since I have previously helped secure assets belonging to organizations and government entities such as USA (NASA), the Netherlands Governmentv(Dutch Gov.), and various Indian Government departments, I felt a responsibility to report the issue through the proper channels.

⚖️ Responsible Disclosure

Before submitting the report, I carefully explained that I had not exploited the vulnerability in any way.

I also stated that I fully respect the cyber laws and regulations of countries such as Russia, China, Japan, and other nations that maintain strict policies regarding unauthorized security testing and cannot allow other country's hackers to hunting on his websites

My intention was not to test beyond what was necessary to verify the issue, but simply to help improve security through responsible disclosure.

After preparing a detailed report, I submitted the information to the relevant contact point and waited for a response 📧

📩 The Response

To my surprise, I received a reply from their security team.

They thanked me for reporting the issue and informed me that the details had been forwarded to the relevant team for review and remediation ✅🎉

Receiving an acknowledgment from a Russian Government entity was something I had never expected, especially considering the strict regulations surrounding security testing within the country.

🏅 Final Outcome

  • 📧 Vulnerability reported responsibly
  • ⚖️ Respect for local cyber laws clearly communicated
  • 📩 Official response received from the security team
  • 🔧 Issue forwarded for review and remediation
